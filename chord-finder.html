Chord finder

Pick a root note and a chord type, and this page gathers every way this site knows to play that chord, ordered along the neck: the open version when there is one, the barre shapes, the triads, and the power chords, each group under its own level 2 heading and each voicing under a level 3 heading. This is useful when a lesson names a chord and you want your options immediately.

The web address updates as you choose, so you can bookmark or share a specific chord.
