Triads

A triad is a three-note chord made of the root, the third, and the fifth, played on three neighboring strings. The third decides whether the chord is major or minor. Major and minor triads each come in three inversions. The root is the lowest note in root position, the third is lowest in first inversion, and the fifth is lowest in second inversion.

The shapes are grouped by strings, from the thinnest three to the thickest three. Strings 3 to 5 use the same grips as strings 4 to 6, one string thinner. Every shape is movable, and each one is a level 3 heading followed by one example at real frets.
